## 🚀 Main Projects

### Redforge

Open-source tool designed as a lightweight alternative to Odoo, aimed at business operations management with a modular and minimalist approach.

- **Technologies**: Python, Django, PostgreSQL, Docker
- **Additional Modules**: CRM, invoice management, warehouse tracking, and reporting, available as extensions to expand core functionality.
- **Goal**: Provide a simple and efficient solution for business management without the complexity of larger suites like Odoo.

### Janus

Open-source financial forecasting platform based on Delta Lake and Docker.

- **Technologies**: Python, Delta Lake, PostgreSQL, Docker, yfinance
- **Features**: Collection and analysis of financial data, integration with online archives such as Google Drive and Nextcloud.
- **Goal**: Make financial forecasting accessible to small businesses.
